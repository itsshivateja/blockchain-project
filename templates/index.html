Why decentralised lending?

The project targets common limitations in traditional lending.

1.4B

Financial exclusion

The presentation cites 1.4 billion adults worldwide without a bank account (World Bank Global Findex, 2021).

Days → Weeks

Slow processing

Traditional credit checks, approval, and disbursal can take days to weeks.

Open rules

Pricing transparency

On-chain rules make transaction execution and protocol logic publicly inspectable.

How the platform works

Five stages in the pool-based lending flow.

1

Supply

Lenders deposit assets into the pool and receive interest-bearing tokens.

2

Collateralise

Borrowers lock crypto collateral worth more than the loan.

3

Borrow

Borrow up to the allowed loan-to-value ratio, without paperwork.

4

Repay

Repay principal plus variable interest to unlock collateral.

5

Liquidate

If health factor drops below one, liquidators repay debt and earn a bonus.
